A few things we wish we knew before going — where to stay, what to eat, how to get around, visa, costs, shopping & the best photo spots.

Where We Stayed

01

We mostly chose Airbnbs instead of hotels — they were more affordable and often more convenient for a road trip.

₹3,000–₹4,000 / night

Our average for a decent stay. For a self-drive trip, we’d prioritise parking, location and easy check-in over fancy hotel facilities.

Food — For Vegetarians

02

Tunisians are incredibly hospitable — sometimes they may add toppings you didn't ask for while trying to be helpful 😅 We ended up vetting a ham pizza after clearly saying no chicken. So always double-check your order!

Getting Around

03

We preferred self-drive. It gave us the freedom to cover multiple places without depending on public transport.

  • 🚘 Rental car: Best for a road trip
  • 🚕 Taxis: Easy and affordable for shorter distances
  • 🚌 Buses / shared taxis: Cheap options for intercity travel
Rental tip: Book early during peak season and check the car's year and condition before accepting it. We’d pick a newer, well-maintained car over saving a little on the rental.

Visa for Indians

04

We paid ₹7,000 per person when we travelled.

We submitted our documents to the nearest Tunisian Embassy / Consulate and received our visa within 2 weeks at most.

Important: Online visa information can be inaccurate or outdated. We recommend calling the embassy directly for the latest requirements before applying.

📄 Cover letter
✈️ Flight + accommodation bookings
