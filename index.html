🇹🇳 Our 10-Day
Tunisia Road Trip

Our practical first-timer route through Tunisia — beaches, old towns, Roman history, Djerba and the option to add the Sahara if you have extra days or are travelling in cooler months.

✈️ Includes flights🚗 Self-drive 🏖️ Beaches🏛️ History🌴 Djerba

🗺️ Our Route

Tunis→Sousse→ Monastir→Kairouan→ El Jem→ Djerba→Douz / Tozeur*→ Tunis→Carthage→Sidi Bou Said

*The desert region is an optional add-on — see the Sahara section below.

✈️ Before You Go

If you're planning a Tunisia trip, save this itinerary! 📌 We explored Tunisia by our own rental car, which made it much easier to cover different places at our own pace.

🚗 Best for this route
Self-driving gives you flexibility to stop, explore smaller towns and travel at your own pace.
🌡️ Think about the season
We travelled in August. The heat was intense, so we skipped the overnight Sahara camp experience.
Important: Visa/entry rules, flight schedules, rental requirements and prices can change. Verify current information before booking.

📅 Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 1 — TUNIS → SOUSSE 🚗
  • ✈️ Land at Tunis Airport
  • 🚗 Pick up rental car & drive to Sousse
  • 🏨 Check-in + relax
  • 🌊 Easy evening to recover from the journey
DAY 2 — SOUSSE 🌊
  • 🏛️ Explore Sousse Medina
  • 🌴 Walk around the city
  • 🌅 Evening at Port El Kantaoui
  • 🍽️ Relaxed dinner by the marina
DAY 3 — Ribat of Monastir + KAIROUAN + EL JEM 🏛️
  • 🚗 Sousse → Monastir
  • 🏛️ Visit the Ribat of Monastir
  • 🚗 Continue to Kairouan and explore nearby Medinas
  • 🚗 Continue to EL JEM
  • 🏛️ Visit the famous El Jem Amphitheatre
  • 🕌 Explore the old city & important sights
  • 🚗 Back to Sousse
DAY 4 — SOUSSE → DJERBA 🚗
⚠️ Long driving day: Keep the schedule light.
  • 🚗 Drive towards Djerba and explore Matmata on your way
  • 🏨 Check-in at your hotel
  • 😴 Relax and enjoy the evening
DAY 5 — DJERBA 🎨
  • 🎨 Explore Djerbahood
  • 🏺 Visit Guellala
  • 🛍️ Explore local markets (Houmt Souk)
  • 🌴 Take it slow and enjoy the island
DAY 6 — DJERBA 🌴
  • 🏖️ Explore nearby beaches
  • 🛍️ Local markets & souks
  • 📸 Explore more of the island
  • 🌅 Sunset by the coast
DAY 7 — DJERBA → DOUZ / TOZEUR 🏜️ OPTIONAL

Choose based on your interests and the season.

  • 🚗 Head towards the desert region
  • ✨ Explore Douz or Tozeur
  • 🏕️ Desert / camp experience is an option
Our experience: We travelled in August and it was extremely hot, so we skipped the Sahara/camp experience. If you're visiting in cooler months, consider adding it.
DAY 8 — DOUZ / TOZEUR → TUNIS 🚗
  • 🚗 Long drive back towards Tunis
  • 🏨 Check-in & relax

If you're skipping the Sahara, use these days differently rather than forcing a desert stop into a hot-weather trip.

DAY 9 — TUNIS + CARTHAGE + SIDI BOU SAID 💙
  • 🏛️ Explore Tunis
  • 🏺 Visit ancient Carthage
  • 💙 Explore the beautiful blue & white streets of Sidi Bou Said
  • 🌅 Stay for sunset
DAY 10 — TUNIS → INDIA ✈️
  • ☕ Last breakfast / quick exploration
  • 🚗 Return rental car
  • ✈️ Head to Tunis Airport & fly back home ❤️

🏨 Where to Stay

Nights 1–3
Sousse
Nights 4–6
Djerba
Night 7*
Douz / Tozeur
Nights 8–9*
Tunis

*If you add the Sahara/desert section. If you skip it, you can adjust the final nights around your flight and preferred pace.

🚗 Our Biggest Tip: Rent a Car!

For us, driving ourselves was one of the best parts of the trip. It gave us the freedom to explore smaller towns, stop whenever we wanted and cover much more of Tunisia without depending on public transport.

Freedom
Stop whenever a place catches your eye.
Flexibility
Build your days around your own pace.

🏜️ Sahara Trip — ADD-ON

Have a few extra days or travelling in winter? Consider extending the trip into the Sahara.

Option 1 — Douz
Use Douz as your desert gateway and add a desert experience/camp.
Option 2 — Tozeur
Explore the Tozeur desert region if landscapes and desert excursions are your priority.
Our August note: We skipped the overnight Sahara/camp because of the extreme heat. Cooler months can make this add-on much more appealing.
