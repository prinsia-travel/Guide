Travel Itinerary

🗺️ Our Route

Istanbul→Cappadocia→Antalya→Fethiye / Ölüdeniz→Istanbul

Rental car for the road-trip portion, then public transport to explore Istanbul at the end.

✈️ Before You Go

This itinerary combines city exploring, long drives, landscapes and beach time. Keep the route flexible around flights, weather and how you feel after each journey.

🚗 Best for this routeSelf-driving gives you flexibility to stop, explore smaller places and travel at your own pace. Confirm one-way rental rules and drop-off arrangements before booking.
🎈 Think about the seasonCappadocia balloon flights depend on weather and official permissions. If seeing the balloons is important, leave a backup morning when possible.
ImportantRoad times, opening hours, transport schedules and boat-party inclusions can change. Verify current details before booking.

🌆 Istanbul · Arrival Day

We kept the first day easy to recover from jet lag, then went out to get our first feel of the city.

ARRIVAL DAY — REST & ISTIKLAL STREET 🌙
  • ✈️ Arrive in Istanbul and collect your luggage.
  • 🏨 Head to the hotel and rest for around 6–7 hours to recover from jet lag.
  • 🚶 In the evening, explore Istiklal Street and the surrounding lanes.
  • ☕ Enjoy cafés, Turkish snacks, shopping streets and the lively atmosphere.
  • 🌃 Take a relaxed walk and enjoy the city after dark.
💡 Little tipIstiklal Street is lively late into the night, but individual shops and cafés have different closing times. Check ahead if there’s a particular place you want to visit.

🎈 Cappadocia

Fairy chimneys, cave-hotel vibes, underground cities and the iconic sunrise balloons.

TRAVEL DAY — ISTANBUL → CAPPADOCIA 🚗
  • Pick up your rental car in the morning.
  • Start the road trip towards Cappadocia.
  • Arrive in Göreme, check in and rest.

It’s a long drive, so allow most of the day for travel, fuel and rest stops.

EXPLORE DAY 1 — VALLEYS & SUNSET
  • Visit Göreme Open-Air Museum.
  • Explore Paşabağ (Monk’s Valley) and its fairy chimneys.
  • Walk around Çavuşin Village.
  • Watch sunset at Red Valley.
EXPLORE DAY 2 — BALLOONS & HISTORY 🎈
  • Take a sunrise hot-air balloon ride, weather permitting.
  • Or watch the balloons from a scenic viewpoint.
  • Visit Uçhisar Castle for panoramic views.
  • Explore Kaymaklı or Derinkuyu Underground City.
  • If time allows, walk a section of Ihlara Valley.
EXPLORE DAY 3 — POTTERY & LANDSCAPES
  • Visit Love Valley and Devrent Valley.
  • Explore Zelve Open-Air Museum.
  • Head to Avanos for a pottery workshop.
  • Enjoy Göreme’s cafés, shops and scenic views.
🎈 Don’t missWatching the balloons rise over Cappadocia at sunrise. Balloon flights depend on weather and official permissions, so a backup morning is useful.

🌊 Antalya

One full day for a waterfall, Mediterranean views and a little time to unwind before the next drive.

DÜDEN WATERFALLS 💦
  • Travel from Cappadocia to Antalya.
  • Visit Düden Waterfalls and enjoy the natural scenery.
  • Take time for photographs and a relaxed walk.
KONYAALTI BEACH 🩵
  • Head to Konyaaltı Beach for Mediterranean views.
  • Walk along the coast and spend some time by the water.
  • Enjoy dinner locally and rest before continuing to Ölüdeniz.
💡 Planning noteThe drive from Cappadocia is long. Keep plans flexible if you arrive late, rather than squeezing every activity into one evening.

🏖️ Fethiye & Ölüdeniz

Turquoise water, dramatic mountains, paragliders and a boat day with a party atmosphere.

DAY 1 — ANTALYA → ÖLÜDENIZ
  • Drive towards Fethiye and Ölüdeniz.
  • Visit Ölüdeniz Beach and admire the turquoise water.
  • Relax at Belcekız Beach or visit the Blue Lagoon if time and access allow.
  • Watch paragliders land near the beach.
  • Enjoy a relaxed evening by the coast.
DAY 2 — BOAT TRIP & COASTAL STOPS 🚤
  • Board a boat excursion departing from the Ölüdeniz area.
  • Enjoy music, swimming and scenic coastal stops.
  • Visit bays and swimming spots according to the boat’s route.
  • Return to shore after a full day on the Mediterranean.
TRIP HIGHLIGHT
Ölüdeniz boat experience

🎉 Foam Party on the Water

Some boat excursions combine coastal swimming stops with a DJ and a foam party on deck. Dance, cool off in the sea and enjoy a lively day out.

  • Confirm your chosen boat trip includes a foam party; not every excursion offers one.
  • Check departure point, duration, inclusions and return time before booking.
  • Bring swimwear, sunscreen, a towel and a waterproof phone pouch.
✨ If you have extra timeExplore Fethiye’s marina and old town, spend another day at Ölüdeniz Beach, or consider tandem paragliding with a licensed operator.

🚋 Istanbul · Final 3 Days

Return via Dalaman, fly back to Istanbul, drop off the rental car according to your booking and explore the city using trams, metro and ferries.

DAY 1 — SULTANAHMET & HISTORY 🕌
  • Explore Sultanahmet Square.
  • Visit the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia.
  • Discover the Basilica Cistern.
  • Visit Topkapi Palace if time and opening hours permit.
  • Finish with Turkish food or dinner overlooking the Bosphorus.
DAY 2 — BALAT & GALATA TOWER 📸
  • Start early: Explore Balat’s colourful streets and historic houses.
  • Photograph the distinctive façades and neighbourhood lanes.
  • Head to Galata Tower and explore its surrounding streets.
  • Walk along Istiklal Street and explore Taksim Square.
  • If time permits, visit Dolmabahçe Palace.
  • Enjoy an evening around the Bosphorus.
📸 Photography tipBalat and the Galata Tower area can get crowded later in the morning and during the day. Arrive early for quieter streets and cleaner photo frames.
DAY 3 — MARKETS & LOCAL LIFE 🛍️
  • Explore the Grand Bazaar.
  • Visit the Spice Bazaar.
  • Discover local cafés and neighbourhood streets.
  • Use trams, metro and ferries to get around like a local.
  • Shop for souvenirs and enjoy a final Turkish meal before heading home.
GETTING AROUND 🚋
  • Use public transport for central Istanbul sightseeing.
  • Plan routes around attraction opening times and your hotel location.
  • Leave buffer time for airport transfers and traffic.

A rental car is usually less convenient than public transport in busy central areas.

🧳 Before You Go — Quick Tips

🎈 Balloon plansBook in advance, but remember flights depend on weather and official permissions.
🚗 Road-trip timingCheck drive times, fuel stops, one-way rental rules and the exact drop-off location.
🌊 Boat-day essentialsPack swimwear, sunscreen, a towel and waterproof protection for your phone.
🎉 Foam-party bookingConfirm the party is included in your chosen Ölüdeniz boat excursion before paying.
📸 Best photo windowsVisit Balat and Galata Tower early for fewer people in your pictures.
🚋 Istanbul transportUse trams, metro and ferries to explore the city centre more easily.

🗺️ The Route at a Glance

Istanbul → Cappadocia → Antalya → Fethiye & Ölüdeniz → Istanbul

  • Istanbul: Arrival, jet-lag recovery and Istiklal Street.
  • Cappadocia: Balloons, fairy chimneys, valleys and cave-hotel vibes.
  • Antalya: Düden Waterfalls and Konyaaltı Beach.
  • Fethiye & Ölüdeniz: Turquoise beaches, boat trip and foam-party fun.
  • Istanbul: Three days of history, photography, markets and public transport.

Transport idea: rent a car for the road-trip portion, then travel from the Fethiye area to Dalaman Airport and fly to Istanbul. Compare alternatives and check one-way rental fees before finalising plans.
