🗺️ The Route

Bangkok→Krabi→Phuket→Delhi

9 days · 3 destinations · City, coast and island time

🌆 Bangkok / 3 days

01 · RIVER VIEWS & NIGHTLIFE 🌃
🚢 ฿1,000–1,200 cruise🏨 Sukhumvit / Silom
  • Check in, then explore ICONSIAM.
  • Book the Chao Phraya Princess Dinner Cruise — buffet, live music and riverside views. Check your departure pier.
  • Night out: Khao San Road, or skyline drinks at Tichuca / Octave.
02 · WEEKEND MARKET & RESET 🛍️
🗓️ Weekend only💆 Massage break
  • Spend the day browsing Chatuchak Weekend Market — clothes, souvenirs, homewares and street food.
  • Wear comfy shoes; the market is huge, so leave time to wander.
  • Recharge with a Thai or foot massage at a reputable spa.
03 · TEMPLES, SKYLINE & SHOPPING 🛕
🎟️ Grand Palace ~฿500🌇 SkyWalk ~฿1,000
  • Morning: Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew. Cover shoulders and knees.
  • Midday: Ferry to Wat Arun (about ฿5 + ~฿200 entry). Thai outfits and photographers may be available nearby.
  • Before sunset: Mahanakhon SkyWalk for day-to-night city views.
  • Shop: Terminal 21 or Platinum Fashion Mall.

Extra-time alternatives: Bubble in the Forest, Nasatta Park or Space & Time Cube Café. Pick one rather than rushing all three.

🏝️ Krabi / 2 days

Stay near Ao Nang for easy access to the beach, tours and evening food spots.

04 · CHECK IN & CHASE THE SUNSET 🌅
🚕 Ride ~฿500–600 return
  • Check in near Ao Nang Beach.
  • Head to Khao Thong Hill for panoramic views (roughly 40 minutes from Ao Nang).
  • Arrive early for sunset seating; finish with dinner at a sea-view café.
05 · ISLANDS, KAYAKING & FIRE SHOW 🚤
🛶 Kayak ~฿200–300/hr🔥 Ao Nang fire show
  • Book an island-hopping tour from a local operator. We chose a 4 Islands tour — confirm the exact route and inclusions.
  • Alternative for a slower day: kayak at Klong Root (Klong Nam Sai) or Ao Thalane.
  • For next time, we’d choose a sunset kayaking tour rather than squeeze in too much.
  • Check out the Ao Nang Beach fire show if running that evening.

🌊 Phuket / 4 days

06 · KRABI → PHUKET 🚌
🚐 2.5–3 hrs฿700–900/person*
  • Transfer to Phuket and check in around Patong, Kata or Karon.
  • Relax at Patong, Kata or Freedom Beach.
  • Night choice: Bangla Road for nightlife or a quiet beachside dinner.
07 · EARLY-MORNING PHI PHI 🚤
🌅 Sunrise tour🍱 Lunch may be included
  • Choose an early speedboat tour to enjoy the islands before peak crowds.
  • Possible stops: Maya Bay, Monkey Beach, Phi Phi Leh / Don and snorkelling spots — route depends on operator and conditions.
  • Confirm park fees, pickup, lunch and equipment before paying.
  • Return, rest and head out for a beach-café dinner.
08 · LOTUS POND, BEACH & OLD TOWN 🌺
  • Visit Maa Doo Bua Café for its lotus-pond setting and photos; check booking and photo-package details.
  • Spend some time at Mai Khao Beach.
  • Explore Phuket Old Town — colourful streets, cafés and local shops.
  • Browse the Old Town night market if it’s operating that day.
09 · SLOW MORNING & FLY HOME ✨
💆 Thai / head massage
  • Enjoy a relaxed breakfast or café stop at Cafe Phuket View Point, Rabbit Hole Phuket or The Cozy Cafe.
  • Fit in a final massage or beach visit if time allows.
  • Check out and leave enough time for the airport transfer.
  • Fly back to Delhi — if the flight is early, move the beach plans to Day 8.
A little Prinsiago reminder

Leave space for the unplanned. ♡

  • 🌅 Prioritise your must-dos instead of racing through every stop.
  • 🌊 Keep boat plans flexible around weather and sea conditions.
  • 🍜 Make time for street food, beach breaks and a massage.

💡 Quick Travel Tips

🎟️ PricesAll prices are approximate. Confirm current rates, fees and inclusions before booking.
🚤 ToursCheck the exact island route, pickup point, park fees and weather policy.
🛕 TemplesCover shoulders and knees; follow local signs and rules.
🛍️ Market daysChatuchak is a weekend market. Verify dates and opening hours.
🛵 Getting aroundUse licensed transport; only rent a scooter if properly licensed and insured.
🏨 Where to stayBangkok: Sukhumvit / Silom · Krabi: Ao Nang · Phuket: Patong for nightlife, Kata/Karon for beach time.

*Transport prices vary by season, operator, route and pickup point. Check details directly before travel.
